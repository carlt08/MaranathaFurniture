Delivery and collection

We have our own delivery vehicle. Ask us about local delivery for your furniture.

How delivery works

  1. Choose delivery when you send your order request, or message us on WhatsApp.
  2. Our staff confirm that the items are in stock and confirm the delivery cost for your address.
  3. Once you accept the final amount, you pay by EFT. We send our banking details.
  4. We arrange a delivery time with you.

Collection

You can also choose to collect your furniture from our store in Porterville once your order is confirmed.
